**Clients** is the list of everyone who's booked with you, with a detail page for each one where you can see their full history, their bonos, and leave private notes.

## The list

**Clients** shows every client you have, with a search box by **name, email, or phone**. Each card shows how many appointments they've had and the date of their last visit.

## A client's page

Opening a client shows:

- **Upcoming appointments** — the ones they have scheduled from now on.
- **History** — all their past appointments, with the result (Completed, No-show, Cancelled) and payment status.
- **Summary** — total appointments, how many were completed, no-shows, and cancelled, plus their first and last visit.
- **Contact** — name, email, and phone; click **Edit** to correct any of these.
- **Bonos** — if the client has purchased session bonos, you'll see how many sessions are left on each one (e.g. "3/10 used"), with a badge when they're down to their last session or have used it all up. See the **Bonos** guide for selling and managing these packages.
- **Private notes** — internal notes about the client that only you and your team can see; the client never sees these. You can add, edit, or delete notes anytime.

## Client accounts vs. guest bookings

Clients can book with you using an account (signing in with Google or email/password) or as a guest (giving just their name, email, and an optional phone number). Both are confirmed instantly and show up the same way in your Clients list and Calendar — the difference is that a client with an account can log into their own portal to check the status of their appointments without having to message you, while a guest relies on the confirmation emails we send them.
