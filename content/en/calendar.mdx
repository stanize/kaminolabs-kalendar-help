**Calendar** is where you see every booking coming in from your public page, plus where you can create appointments yourself and review cancellation requests from clients.

## The tabs

- **Week** — a weekly grid view (Day and Month views are also available) showing every appointment across your team. This is where you create appointments manually.
- **Upcoming** — a list of your confirmed appointments that are coming up, grouped by day (Today, Tomorrow, and following dates).
- **Clients** — bookings from new or guest clients, with a counter showing how many you have.
- **Cancellations** — cancellation requests sent by clients from their own portal, waiting for your approval.
- **Conflicts** — existing appointments that now overlap with a holiday or time off you've added afterward.

## Bookings are confirmed instantly

Every client booking — whether they're signed in with an account or booking as a guest — is **confirmed instantly** as soon as it's submitted. There's no more 24-hour review window: the appointment shows up right away in Upcoming and in the week view.

What does change depending on how the client booked is the **Clients** tab: that's where bookings from guests and from clients booking with you for the first time show up, with a **Contacted / Confirmed** button to mark that you've already called or messaged them to confirm the details in person — something some businesses do as an extra step even though the booking is already confirmed in the system. Use the **All** filter if you only want to see a certain type of client.

## Creating an appointment manually

If a client calls you or walks in without having booked online, you can add the appointment yourself:

1. Go to the **Week** tab and click **Add appointment** on an open slot (or the equivalent button in the view).
2. In the **New appointment** form, choose the **Service** and, if you work as a team, the **Provider**.
3. Adjust the **Date** and **Time** if they're different from the slot you clicked. If the time falls outside your working hours, you'll see a note ("Outside business hours"), but you can still create the appointment.
4. If the slot overlaps a holiday or team time off, you'll see a note naming that closure — you can still proceed if needed.
5. Enter the **Client name** (required) and, optionally, their **Email** and **Phone**.
6. Add internal **Comments** if you want to leave notes about the appointment.
7. Check **Send confirmation email** and/or **Send confirmation via WhatsApp** (this one only shows up if you have WhatsApp booking enabled — see the **WhatsApp** guide) if you want the client to get notified.
8. Click **Create appointment**.

To edit an existing appointment, open it and click **Modify** — it's the same form, titled "Modify appointment" with a **Save changes** button.

## Past appointments: result and payment

Opening an appointment that's already happened lets you record how it went:

- **Result** — mark it as Completed, No-show, or Cancelled.
- **Payment** — mark it as Paid or Pending. Choosing Paid lets you pick the **Payment method**: Cash, Card, or one of the client's active bonos (if they have any — see the **Bonos** guide). If the payment is already applied to a bono, the selector is locked; to change it, go to the Bonos page.

## Cancellations

When a client with an account requests to cancel their appointment from their own portal, the request shows up in the **Cancellations** tab with a "Cancellation requested" badge, instead of being cancelled automatically. From there you can:

- **Approve** — confirms the cancellation; the client is notified by email.
- **Deny** — rejects the request and the appointment stays as it was.

You can also cancel any appointment directly yourself (confirmed or not) without going through this tab, by opening it and clicking **Cancel appointment**.

## Conflicts

If you add a holiday or team time off (see **Availability**) and you already had appointments scheduled in that slot, they'll show up in the **Conflicts** tab along with the holiday or time off they overlap, so you can decide whether to reschedule them or leave them as is.

## Automatic notifications

Every time a new booking comes in, you get an email alert. Your clients also get an email confirming their appointments, and an email if you cancel one.
