**Bonos** lets you sell prepaid session packages to your clients and keep track of how many sessions they have left.

## Bono types

Under the **Bono types** tab you define the templates you'll later sell:

- **Name** — e.g. "10-session bono".
- **Sessions** — how many sessions it includes.
- **Price (€)** — the package's total price.

Click **New bono type** to create one. From the list you can **Edit** any type, or **Deactivate** one you no longer want to sell (without deleting bonos already sold with it) — an inactive type is marked "Inactive" and you can **Activate** it again anytime.

## Selling a bono

Under the **Sold bonos** tab, click **Sell bono**:

1. Search for the **Client** by name, email, or phone.
2. Choose the **Bono type** from your active types (if you have none active, create one first under Bono types).
3. Click **Confirm sale**.

## Tracking sold bonos

Each sold bono shows how many sessions have been used (e.g. "3/10 used") and the date it was sold. There's a badge when they're down to their **last session**, and an **Exhausted** badge once all sessions have been used.

- Filter by client name with the search box.
- Sort by **Most recent** or **Fewest sessions left** — useful for spotting bonos that are about to run out.
- Click **View history** on a bono to see which sessions were used and when.

## Paying for an appointment with a bono

When you mark a past appointment as paid (from Calendar), you can choose one of the client's active bonos as the payment method instead of cash or card — this automatically consumes one session from the bono. If you make a mistake, you can reverse a used session from the bono's history (**Switch to cash** or **Switch to card**), which returns the session to the bono and changes that appointment's payment method.

Once a payment is applied to a bono, that appointment's payment method is locked in Calendar — to change it, do it from here, in Bonos.
